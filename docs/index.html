Pairing two Time On Ice legs from the same team: teammates in the same tier gain or lose minutes together (power plays, overtime and trailing late lift the top guys; blowouts lift the depth guys), while top and depth players trade minutes. So same tier → same direction pairs better, Top vs Depth → opposite directions pairs better, and Mid players are close to independent. Tier = rank in the team's ice-time order over each player's last 10 games (F1–F3 Top, F4–F9 Mid, F10+ Depth; D1–D2 Top, D3–D4 Mid, D5+ Depth). Measured on 2024–26 data; it doesn't make any single leg more likely.
